About

Where people strategy meets delivery

I work at the point where HR decisions and technology delivery meet. Over seven years across UAE federal government, ADNOC's supply chain and private-sector firms, I have led workforce planning and talent acquisition, and delivered the ERP, Microsoft 365 and service platforms those workforces depend on.

That combination is what sets my work apart. I can write the HR policy, configure the workflow that enforces it, and report on the results. My projects range from running HR for a 500-person engineering firm to multi-entity platforms shared across Abu Dhabi government entities.

I hold an MBA and an MCom, and I am certified in PMP, PHR and Lean Six Sigma, with recent work in generative AI and AI automation.
